the diagnostic system

Nine failure classes. 54 subtypes. Every recurring structural failure fits somewhere.

When you send the five-line brief, it is run against the Diagnostic Taxonomy — a classification system developed alongside 10 published papers and refined through three public proof sets. The taxonomy identifies which class of failure is producing the recurring break, and which subtype is the specific mechanism. That classification governs the engagement path, the diagnostic frame, and the correction sequence.

Systems do not fail randomly. They fail in recognisable structural patterns — across sectors, scales, and domains. The taxonomy makes that pattern visible before any diagnosis begins.
