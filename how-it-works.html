How it works

How an engagement runs, from first email to final document.

Three fixed-scope engagements. Written-first. Fixed fee. Every outcome priced in advance.

You know what you’ll receive, when, and what you pay in every outcome, including if the work stops early. No meeting chain by default and no open-ended advisory.

Need an architecture mapped? Start with a Structural Lens request. A failure that keeps returning? Send the Five-Line Brief. I confirm the right scope before any paid work, and redesign never bypasses diagnosis.

working protocol
01 // How we work how we interact

Written-first, asynchronous, and bounded.

written-first async by default fixed process

Most of the work happens through email, document review, and targeted written questions. I do not move into your workflow or begin with a chain of meetings. The aim is simple: keep the problem, scope, and conclusions explicit from start to finish.

Default mode

Email, document review, and written clarification. This keeps the engagement focused on structural signal rather than conversational noise.

Clarifying questions

If something important is missing, I may ask targeted questions. These are used to tighten scope and locate the real boundary, not to create a meeting cycle.

What this prevents

Premature implementation, endless alignment calls, and the slow drift that happens when the work stays verbally defined rather than written down.

before work starts
02 // Before work starts free until you agree the scope

Nothing is charged, and no internal documents are needed, until you’ve agreed a written scope.

01

Send the brief

Five lines about the recurring failure, or a short note about the system you want mapped for a Lens Brief. Only what’s needed to judge fit.

02

Free fit check

I review whether structural diagnosis is appropriate. If it isn’t, I’ll say so. There’s no charge.

03

Written scope

I send the question to be examined, the evidence needed, what you’ll receive, the fee and the cancellation charges. You accept by email.

04

Evidence and first payment

You send the agreed evidence pack. I check the listed items are there, then invoice 50%.

05

Day 1

Work starts when both the evidence and cleared payment have arrived. I confirm day 1 and the delivery date by email.

Not sure which engagement you need? You don’t need to diagnose yourself. Describe the situation and I’ll recommend the right scope before any paid work begins. Compare the three engagements →
your evidence
03 // Your evidence agreed in the scope, sent after you accept

Useful signal, not a polished pack. The written scope lists exactly what’s needed for your case.

The evidence pack usually includes
  • Diagrams, policies, workflows, notes, contracts or screenshots
  • Post-mortems, incident reports or internal documents showing what actually happened
  • A clear statement of known constraints: technical, legal, budgetary or operational
  • Timely written answers if I have questions
What matters most
  • Accuracy over polish. Rough materials are fine if they reflect reality.
  • Operating reality over official narrative. What actually happens, not what the policy says.
  • Boundary signal over volume. Knowing what cannot change is as useful as knowing what keeps breaking.
  • Honest constraints. Hidden constraints discovered mid-engagement create scope problems.
during the work
04 // During the work the 14-day diagnosis

The diagnosis is normally delivered within 14 calendar days. A Lens Brief takes 7.

→

If more evidence is needed

I ask in writing, explaining why and how it affects the date. The clock pauses until it arrives, or for up to 7 days, whichever comes first.

→

If the work can’t responsibly continue

I tell you as soon as that’s clear, normally within the first 7 days, with a short written note explaining why. The total fee becomes £2,000.

→

If the evidence can’t settle it

After the full examination, you receive a written finding: what was established, what remains open and what evidence could resolve it. The total fee is £5,000.

If the work identifies a time-sensitive decision or continuing serious harm, I may send a short preliminary notice before delivery.

after delivery
05 // After delivery review, confirm, redesign
Lens Brief or Diagnostic Artifact
  • You receive the written document by email.
  • Within 14 days, you can send one consolidated round of questions.
  • I reply within 7 days, explaining or correcting the work.
  • The remaining 50% is invoiced on delivery, due within 14 days.
Full Structural Engagement
  • You receive the Diagnostic Artifact first.
  • You have 14 days to confirm it, send your questions or challenge a finding. After my reply, you have at least 7 more days.
  • If two viable directions emerge, I explain both and you choose one.
  • The redesign starts only with your written confirmation (and your choice, if two directions are offered), and takes a further 14 days.
  • You receive the Translation Artifact, Redesign Executive Summary and Full Redesign Artifact, plus one round of questions on the redesign.
  • If you decide not to go ahead, the diagnosis costs £10,000 and £2,500 is refunded.

If I’ve made a material error, I correct it at my expense. If the boundary expands, the problem changes shape or new analysis is needed, that becomes separate work, agreed in writing. Scope creep is never absorbed silently.

what you receive
07 // What you receive a written artifact, not a deck

Every engagement ends with a defined written output, designed to circulate, support decisions and give builders a stable reference point.

Who it’s for

Decision owners, operators and builders who need a shared structural read without reconstructing the logic from meetings or scattered documents.

What it isn’t

Not a slide deck, workshop package, implementation team or open-ended retainer. Scope and output are defined before work begins.

What your team does next

Your team, or a partner you appoint, turns the work into detailed designs, build tasks and operational changes. Implementation, training and ongoing support aren’t included.

For what each document contains, see the Method.

using the work
08 // Using the work rights and confidentiality

Your licence

You can share the documents confidentially for review as soon as you receive them. Once the fee due is paid, you can use, adapt and implement the work permanently in your business, including through contractors working for you. I keep the copyright.

Confidentiality

Your materials stay confidential and are never used as a case study, or to name you, without your written permission. Publishing or reselling the work needs my permission.

Your evidence

The scope names any business AI tools proposed for drafting. Within 30 days of closure, working evidence is removed from my systems and deletion is requested from agreed providers. I keep only a limited engagement record.

when it isn’t a fit
09 // When it isn’t a fit decided free, at the fit check
Not a fit when
  • The issue is too broad or undefined for a bounded written engagement
  • The real need is implementation, delivery leadership or ongoing facilitation
  • The available inputs don’t contain enough signal to define a reliable boundary
  • The failure is mainly operational noise rather than a structural problem
  • Several unrelated failures are being combined into one request
What happens then
  • I say so at the fit check. There’s no charge.
  • If the problem is outside these engagements, I may be able to point you to a more appropriate route.
  • For a Full engagement, three things must be in place: decision authority, access to evidence and a credible implementation route. If they aren’t yet, a standalone Diagnostic may be the right start.
how to start

Send the repeated failure. Not the proposed fix.

The five-line brief. No diagnosis yet. Just the signal.

01Step 01

Describe the problem. Send the Five-Line Brief for a recurring failure, or contact me about a Structural Lens.

02Step 02

Establish fit. I review the initial problem and scope. Qualification is free; it is not a diagnosis.

03Step 03

Agree the work. If suitable, we agree the fixed fee, evidence requirements and written deliverable before paid work begins.

Send these five lines
  1. 01What keeps going wrong?
  2. 02What is it costing? Money, time, trust, load, or drift.
  3. 03Where does it show up? Teams, roles, systems, or touchpoints.
  4. 04What has already been tried?
  5. 05What cannot change? Non-negotiables and constraints.

If the work is not the right fit, I will say so.
