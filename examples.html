Proof Sets

Three systems. One structural method.

Each case presents a complete artifact family produced independently from public evidence and not commissioned by the organisation named. Most contain four deliverables. Patreon / Apple IAP contains five because the locked diagnosis produces two mutually exclusive restricted redesign specifications.

Three artifacts form the public proof layer: Translation Artifact, Diagnostic Artifact, and Redesign Executive Summary. The Full Redesign Artifact carries the builder-grade transition sequence, invariants, recapture gates, and handoff specification and remains restricted.

The point is repeatability. Each case moves through the same structural sequence: system boundary → workflow trace → functional diagnosis → governing diagnosis → correction dependency order. The domain changes; the proof standard does not.

Proof of structural capability, not contracted outcomes.

Shared proof structure

The domain changes. The proof standard does not.

Every case moves through the same public sequence: shared-language translation, evidence-tiered diagnosis, then board-level replacement logic. The restricted Full Redesign Artifact carries the implementation-grade specification.

The proof sets demonstrate method transfer across domains. They do not claim commissioning, implementation, or client outcomes.

Restricted layer

Why the Full Redesign Artifacts are restricted

The public layer is sufficient to show the diagnosis, evidence boundary, replacement logic, and proof standard. The Full Redesign Artifact contains the builder-grade transition sequence, invariants, recapture gates, and handoff specification used in a Full Structural Engagement.
