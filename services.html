  • A build map showing structural implications for sequence, interfaces, and transition logic

03 // Full Structural Engagement
£25,000 Fixed fee

Diagnose the failure. Specify the replacement.

Best when naming the failure isn’t enough and your team needs a written blueprint for what replaces it.

diagnosis + redesign fixed-scope standard 14-day window* builder handoff

Diagnosis comes first. Once it’s confirmed in writing, the engagement specifies the replacement: what the new structure must do, what changes and in what order, and the constraints that stop old workarounds creeping back.

What it answers

  • What must the replacement structure be organised to do?
  • What has to change first, and in what order?
  • Which interfaces and handoffs change, and how?
  • What stops the old pattern from quietly returning?

What you send

The same Five-Line Brief, then the evidence the diagnosis needs. The Diagnostic Artifact is delivered first; the redesign follows once its findings are confirmed in writing.

What you receive

Four linked written artifacts:

  • Diagnostic Artifact: the structural diagnosis and build map
  • Full Redesign Artifact: the written blueprint for the replacement
  • Translation Artifact: the findings in plain language for leadership and teams
  • Redesign Executive Summary: a board-level summary that can circulate more widely than the full blueprint

What your team does next

Your team, or a partner you appoint, turns the blueprint into detailed designs, build tasks and working changes. Implementation isn’t included.

What the blueprint specifies

  • The corrected primitive: what the new structure is organised to do
  • The target boundary: what the new structure covers, and what stays outside it
  • Transition sequence: the order the changes must happen in
  • Dependency order: which changes rely on others being done first
  • Interface and handoff changes across teams, systems and contracts
  • Refusal rules: what the new structure must never allow
  • Recapture gates: checks that stop the old pattern coming back
  • Builder handoff: the requirements your team or partner builds from

Covers one redesign direction. Unusually large systems, such as several institutions or dozens of interfaces, are quoted separately at the fit stage.

how the work is produced

A governed production sequence, not subjective consulting.

The amount of diagnostic machinery depends on the service. A Lens Brief extracts architecture. A Diagnostic Artifact tests the failure against defined conditions and establishes the governing diagnosis where the evidence supports it. Full Structural Engagement carries that confirmed diagnosis into redesign. AI can assist drafting and production; it does not determine the governing diagnosis or correction order.

what’s not included
03 // What’s not included outside scope for all engagements

All three engagements produce fixed-scope written work. Your team or partner carries out the changes.

Delivery and change

The artifact defines what must change. It doesn’t carry it out.

  • ✗Implementation, build work or delivery management
  • ✗Workshops, facilitation, training or coaching
  • ✗Retainers or open-ended advisory
  • ✗Transformation programmes
Technical work

Structural diagnosis, not engineering or incident work.

  • ✗Code debugging or code audits
  • ✗Technical root-cause analysis from logs or infrastructure
  • ✗Live incident response
  • ✗Architecture, infrastructure, security or compliance work
Not a fit

Problems that don’t need structural diagnosis.

  • ✗A one-off bug with a clear technical owner
  • ✗A known defect that just needs fixing
  • ✗A design that’s already settled and only needs building
  • ✗General dissatisfaction without a recurring pattern

If it isn’t structural, I’ll say so.

If the problem is mainly technical, already understood, or can’t be credibly diagnosed from the available evidence, I’ll tell you at the fit stage rather than deliver work that wouldn’t hold. The Full engagement is delivered as one complete set, not as individual parts.

fit and scope
04 // Fit and scope before a full engagement
Before a Full Structural Engagement

Three things must be in place:

  • ✓Someone who can approve major structural decisions
  • ✓Access to the internal evidence the work needs
  • ✓A credible route to implementation, through an existing team or an appointed partner

If these aren’t in place yet, the Diagnostic Artifact on its own may be the right starting point. A diagnosis can help decide whether anything should be built at all.

Structural fit examples

The kind of situation this work is built for:

  • →A platform that recruits one kind of expertise but actually runs on another, so work keeps going to the wrong people and workarounds pile up.
  • →A service where continuity is officially the institution’s job but is actually carried by families and frontline staff.
  • →A product team whose fixes don’t hold sprint after sprint, because the rule governing the failure has never been named.
  • →An organisation where builders make design decisions mid-build, because the underlying architecture was never settled.

delivery terms
06 // Delivery terms after the artifact is delivered

Clarification pass

One minor clarification pass is included for each applicable delivery stage. This covers clarification of the document as delivered, not new analysis, extended scope, or additional research.

For the Full Structural Engagement, one clarification pass applies to the Diagnostic Artifact and one to the Full Redesign Artifact. The Translation Artifact and Redesign Executive Summary are derived from those locked frames and do not require separate clarification passes.

When new work is required

If the boundary expands, the analysis deepens, or the problem changes shape after delivery, that becomes a separate engagement. Scope creep is not absorbed silently.

In the Full Structural Engagement, the Diagnostic Artifact is delivered first. Redesign is released only after the diagnostic frame is confirmed in writing.

The Full Structural Engagement runs in a standard 14-day window, subject to timely client inputs and diagnostic confirmation. If client-side delay prevents timely confirmation of the diagnostic frame, final redesign release may extend beyond day 14. If the diagnosis shows that redesign is not valid within the engagement boundary, I will say so rather than release a speculative artifact.

Confidentiality

All materials you share are treated as confidential. AI tools are used only on business plans that do not train on your data, with names and personal details removed wherever possible. The artifact is yours. It is not shared, referenced, or used as a case study without your explicit permission.

This is architecture-led work. The diagnostic logic, structural interpretation, and final recommendations are mine. See the Method page for how AI is used in production.
