Terms of Engagement

These terms cover fixed-scope written work for business clients. The written scope identifies what you are commissioning, who can make decisions and the fee. Section 5 sets out what you pay if the work ends early. “Jamie” means the supplier identified in the scope. “You” means the client identified there.

Last updated: 19 November 2026 · Jamie Forrester, Edinburgh, UK · Contact: hello@jamieforrester.com

Scope Three fixed-scope written engagements for business clients: Structural Lens Brief, Diagnostic Artifact, or Full Structural Engagement. Diagnosis comes first; a Full redesign starts only after you confirm it. No implementation or retainers.
Fees 50% upfront, the rest on delivery. If the work ends early, you pay only the fixed amount for the outcome reached. Section 5 sets out every case.
Use and confidentiality Once paid, you have a permanent licence to use and implement the work. Your materials stay confidential, are never used as a case study without permission, and are deleted within 30 days of closure.
terms of engagement
01 // What you can commission what you can commission

Structural Lens Brief · £2,000. A written brief of normally 6–10 pages describing the architecture, functions, boundaries, dependencies and tensions in one system, problem, method or body of work. No failure diagnosis or redesign is included.

Diagnostic Artifact · £10,000. A written examination of one recurring failure. It identifies the governing cause where the evidence supports one, the relevant rules and constraints, and what the findings mean for the order of change. Section 5 also provides for an early stop or an inconclusive finding.

Full Structural Engagement · £25,000. Diagnosis followed, with your written go-ahead, by one redesign direction. You receive the Diagnostic Artifact first. The final set comprises the Translation Artifact, Redesign Executive Summary and Full Redesign Artifact. The Full Redesign Artifact specifies the replacement structure and the sequence and conditions for change. You or your appointed team own detailed implementation decisions and delivery.

Two later redesign commissions are described in section 6. All services are written, remote and fixed in scope. Implementation, programme management, workshops, retainers, ongoing advice and specialist legal, financial, regulatory, security or compliance advice are outside the service. These terms are offered for business or professional purchases. Anyone seeking a service for personal purposes should enquire separately before placing an order. Mandatory legal rights are preserved.

02 // Agreement and start agreement and start

The fit assessment is free. A five-line brief or Lens enquiry starts a conversation. Please send only the information needed for screening. Internal evidence is requested after fit and scope have been discussed.

Jamie sends a written offer identifying both parties and their addresses, the agreed service, fee, deliverables, initial evidence, designated email addresses and the people authorised to accept the offer, confirm the diagnosis and choose a direction. Before you accept, the scope email summarises the upfront payment, delivery stages, cancellation charges and rights to use the work. For a standard Diagnostic or Full engagement it states the £5,000 charge if you voluntarily cancel during diagnosis. The agreement forms when Jamie receives written acceptance of the identified scope and dated terms from your authorised representative. You may replace a representative by written notice from an authorised person.

These terms and the accepted scope form the agreement. A variation must be expressly agreed in writing by both parties. A scope provision prevails where it identifies and replaces a specific provision here. A purchase order may provide a billing reference. Any additional purchasing or portal terms require express written agreement. A data-processing agreement takes priority on the processing matters it expressly governs. Earlier engagements retain their agreed terms.

You supply the agreed minimum evidence pack or Lens source material. Jamie checks that the listed items are present and then invoices the first 50%. This initial check does not assess whether the evidence will establish a diagnosis. Day 1 is the day both the minimum pack and cleared payment have been received. Jamie confirms day 1 and the target delivery date by email. If the pack is incomplete, Jamie identifies what is missing. Unless you agree an extension, the engagement closes if it remains incomplete 14 calendar days after that notice. Any advance received before work starts is returned in full.

03 // Delivery and evidence timing, evidence and early outcomes

All periods are calendar days unless marked as working days. Working days are Monday to Friday excluding Scottish bank holidays. Day 1 counts towards a production period. A response period starts the day after receipt and ends at 23:59 UK time on its final day.

The normal production period is seven days for a Lens Brief and 14 days for a diagnosis, subject to agreed variations and the evidence pauses below. For Full, allow a further 14 days for redesign from written confirmation of the diagnosis and, if required, selection of a direction. Client review time falls between the two stages. Jamie confirms the start of redesign in writing when substantive redesign work begins. Its delivery clock runs from the required client approval and choice, regardless of when that start notice is sent.

During diagnosis or redesign, Jamie may request specific additional evidence essential to the agreed work. The written request explains why it is needed, what work is affected and the revised timing. The affected production clock pauses from the request until sufficient material is received or seven days elapse, whichever occurs first, unless an extension is agreed in writing. Jamie then resumes on available evidence, explains any limits and confirms the revised date. Overlapping pauses count once. Jamie’s rework and capacity do not pause the clock. Material new facts or requests that change the agreed task require written agreement on any change to price or timing.

If Jamie establishes that the diagnosis cannot responsibly proceed, Jamie tells you as soon as that is established, normally within the first seven days of diagnostic work, and delivers a short closure note explaining what was examined, why further work cannot responsibly proceed and what would need to change. The same early-stop fee applies if that limitation first becomes apparent later and the full examination has not been completed. An inconclusive finding requires a completed examination and a written account of what can be established, what remains unresolved and what evidence could decide the question. Missing records and competing explanations can both lead to that result.

For a Lens Brief, Jamie checks the material during the first two working days. If it clearly cannot support the brief, Jamie tells you and returns the advance. If a material deficiency emerges later, you may agree to supplement the material and revise the date. If a usable brief cannot be delivered, the engagement closes with a full refund. Diagnostic evidence pauses and diagnostic outcome fees do not apply to the Lens.

Jamie may send a preliminary notice if the work identifies a time-sensitive decision or continuing serious harm. It will identify what has been established and its limits. This service provides no continuous monitoring or emergency response.

04 // Review and the decision to proceed review and confirmation

Jamie delivers by email to the designated recipient, with an attachment or accessible download. Delivery occurs when the message reaches the designated mailbox with the agreed work accessible. A bounced message or inaccessible link starts no payment or response deadline. Report access problems promptly. Jamie will restore access and confirm the applicable date.

Each Lens Brief and Diagnostic Artifact includes one consolidated written clarification request within 14 days of delivery. The Full Redesign Artifact includes one further round after release, covering any consequential corrections to the Translation Artifact and Redesign Executive Summary. Jamie answers within seven days, explaining or correcting the work within scope. A clarification does not delay an otherwise valid invoice. New investigations or changed requirements are priced only by written agreement.

Confirming Full redesign. The named decision-maker has 14 days from receipt of the diagnosis to confirm its version in writing, request clarification or raise a reasoned challenge. Confirmation accepts the diagnosis as a sound basis to proceed. If two materially different directions are supported, Jamie explains both and what choosing each entails. You have 14 days from receiving both choices to select one. Neither silence nor approval of the diagnosis alone authorises a choice on your behalf.

A reasoned challenge identifies the disputed finding and the evidence or reason for questioning it. One consolidated challenge is included on either diagnostic route. Jamie responds within seven days. If a material correction takes longer, Jamie explains why within that period and proposes a written completion date. On Full, the client has at least seven days after receiving the substantive answer or revised diagnosis to decide, or the remainder of the original period if longer. The client must identify the version being confirmed. No deadline for silence expires while a timely point awaits Jamie’s answer.

If confirmation or a required choice is still outstanding at the applicable deadline, Jamie sends one written reminder giving seven final days from receipt. It states the fee, refund and later commissioning price. If the final period expires without the required decision, the engagement closes at the diagnosis-only outcome in section 5. You may decline earlier. If Jamie concludes redesign is unjustified or unsupported, Jamie explains why. The parties may agree a revised scope within 14 days of that notice. Otherwise the diagnosis-only outcome applies.

Errors. Jamie corrects a material error in the agreed work at no charge, including an error introduced by a correction or carried into a related artifact. The routine clarification limit does not prevent a genuine error being raised later. Where a diagnostic error cannot be corrected to a sound diagnosis, the inconclusive fee applies only if Jamie delivers a usable completed inconclusive finding. If Jamie’s error leaves neither usable result, all diagnostic or Full fees paid are refunded. If final redesign has already been released when an error is discovered, Jamie also identifies affected outputs, advises the client which version to stop relying on, and corrects them. Contractual refunds and other available remedies are preserved.

06 // Later redesign work later redesign commissions

Where a completed, sound diagnosis supports redesign, you may commission a separate redesign within 90 days of closure, including where Full closed without redesign. The new fee is £25,000, taking the overall fee with the £10,000 diagnosis to £35,000. It includes rechecking the diagnosis and producing the Translation Artifact, Redesign Executive Summary and Full Redesign Artifact for one direction. The option is exercised by written acceptance of the later scope within the 90 days. Tell Jamie promptly if you intend to return so there is time for the recheck and scope.

Before acceptance, Jamie asks what has changed. Routine rechecking is included in the price. If a known change clearly undermines the diagnosis, Jamie explains why and does not invoice for redesign. Fresh investigation requires a separate quote and acceptance. If a material change emerges after acceptance, work pauses for a written decision. If the routine recheck shows the diagnosis cannot support the commissioned redesign, Jamie explains why, closes the later commission and refunds its advance in full unless a revised scope is agreed. You may need to resupply evidence deleted since closure.

If a completed redesign involved a choice between two evidenced directions, you may commission the unchosen direction for £15,000 within 90 days after closure of that engagement. Jamie reuses the confirmed diagnosis and relevant work. The second direction is commissioned separately after the first. After either 90-day period expires, a new Full engagement with a fresh diagnosis may be scoped.

Each later scope identifies the outputs, confirmed source version and selected direction. The standard period is 14 days from cleared advance and receipt of the required materials, confirmation and choice. One clarification round applies. Half is invoiced upfront and half on delivery. If the client voluntarily cancels after substantive redesign begins and before final delivery, Jamie retains only the advance (£12,500 on the £25,000 route or £7,500 on the £15,000 route). Before work starts the advance is returned in full. If Jamie cannot deliver a usable redesign set, Jamie returns the later commission’s advance in full. These outcomes affect only the later commission. Jamie repeats the early-closure amounts in its written scope before purchase. The fees for a new diagnostic examination in section 5 do not automatically apply to a redesign-only commission.

Jamie retains copyright in the artifacts and all existing rights in the practice’s frameworks, writing, templates, diagnostic methods and taxonomies. Third-party rights remain with their owners.

From delivery, you may circulate work confidentially to the people reasonably needed to review it, seek advice, raise a challenge or approve payment. Once the fee due for the outcome reached is paid in full, you have a perpetual, royalty-free, non-exclusive licence to use, copy, adapt and implement the delivered work for your own business. This includes using embedded framework material as needed for those purposes and sharing with advisers, implementation partners and contractors acting for you under confidentiality obligations. You are responsible for their permitted use. Any exceptional circulation restriction must be identified in the scope before purchase.

External publication, resale, public attribution to Jamie or use of the artifacts in marketing requires Jamie’s prior written consent. Jamie accepts professional responsibility to the contracting client alone. A permitted recipient seeking independent reliance must obtain Jamie’s written agreement. Jamie may use general skills and knowledge while respecting confidentiality. Jamie needs your explicit prior written permission to name you, publicise the engagement or use your material or findings as a case study.

08 // Confidentiality and handling evidence confidentiality and data

Each party protects confidential information received from the other and uses it only for the engagement, its administration or the exercise of rights under the agreement. Access is limited to people and providers who need it and owe appropriate confidentiality duties. Permitted recipients include agreed technical providers, confidential professional advisers, accountants and insurers as reasonably necessary. Neither party may use confidential information for unrelated purposes. These obligations continue while the information remains confidential.

The duties exclude information already lawfully known to the recipient, independently developed, lawfully obtained without confidentiality restrictions, or publicly available through no breach. A party required by law to disclose information will limit disclosure to what is required and, where lawful and practicable, notify the other party first.

You confirm you are authorised to share and permit the agreed use of supplied material. Supply the minimum necessary and identify disclosure restrictions in advance. Avoid identifiable patient information, special-category data and criminal-offence data. Any exceptional need for sensitive data requires separate written safeguards in advance. If such information arrives unexpectedly, Jamie will restrict access, notify you and agree secure return or deletion. Special-category data will not be entered into AI tools.

Jamie may use the business workspaces identified in the accepted scope for drafting and production under commercial terms excluding training on client data. The scope identifies any proposed use of Claude Team or ChatGPT Business and any alternative agreed with you. Jamie removes identifying details wherever possible and is responsible for the professional judgments, diagnosis, redesign and correction order. Tell Jamie before acceptance if your policies restrict AI, providers or processing locations. A material change of provider or processing arrangement requires advance written agreement.

Where Jamie processes personal data on your behalf, the parties must agree a processing schedule before transfer. It must address the instructions, data, purpose and duration, confidentiality, security, authorised subprocessors and transfers, incident notification, assistance, audits and return or deletion at your choice, subject to applicable law. Jamie will notify you without undue delay of a personal data breach affecting data processed on your behalf. The Privacy Policy explains processing for Jamie’s own business purposes.

Within 30 days of closure Jamie removes client-supplied evidence and working copies containing it from active systems under Jamie’s control and initiates deletion from agreed providers, including relevant AI chats, project files and separately saved uploads. Protected residual backups are restricted from ordinary use and expire under the applicable backup cycle. If restored, the deletion is reapplied. On a declined enquiry, any unsolicited source evidence is handled on the same timetable from the decline. An agreed processing schedule or legal preservation duty governs where applicable.

Jamie retains only the engagement record reasonably required to document instructions, acceptance, delivery, decisions and payment, including final artifacts where justified. Accounting records are kept for the applicable statutory period. Other retained records are access-restricted, reviewed and deleted when their documented purpose ends. Evidence required for an actual legal hold is isolated and deleted when the duty ends. A subsequent commission or correction may require you to supply source evidence again.

09 // Professional responsibility and liability responsibility and liability

Jamie will perform the agreed services with the reasonable skill and care of an experienced independent systems architect. Findings reflect the evidence and constraints within scope. You are responsible for providing accurate information, identifying restrictions, notifying material changes and obtaining specialist approvals before implementation. Jamie will raise apparent material inconsistencies a reasonably careful practitioner would identify. Responsibility for a failure caused by defective client inputs is limited to the extent those inputs caused it.

You decide whether and how to implement. Jamie does not guarantee savings, successful implementation or any particular business outcome. Ordinary implementation expenditure, the costs of departing from the specification and the consequences of later changes are your responsibility to the extent they arise independently of Jamie’s breach.

Nothing limits liability for fraud or fraudulent misrepresentation, death or personal injury caused by negligence, or liability that cannot lawfully be limited. Subject to those exceptions, Jamie’s total aggregate liability arising from one engagement, in contract, delict including negligence, misrepresentation, breach of statutory duty or otherwise, is capped at the full professional fee originally agreed for that engagement, before any early-closure adjustment and excluding VAT and expenses. Refunds expressly due under section 5 are payable independently of that cap. There is no double recovery for the same loss.

Subject to those exceptions and to the extent legally effective, Jamie excludes indirect or consequential loss and loss of profit, revenue, business opportunity, anticipated savings or goodwill. Reasonable direct costs of identifying and remedying a defect caused by Jamie’s breach remain subject to the aggregate cap. Each party takes reasonable steps to limit avoidable loss.

10 // Interruption and termination interruption and termination

Either party may terminate for material breach by written notice specifying the breach. Where the breach can be remedied, the party in breach has 14 days from receipt to remedy it. An irremediable material breach permits immediate termination. If continuing would be unlawful or would expose confidential material to unauthorised use, the affected work may be suspended immediately while the parties address the issue.

Where events beyond reasonable control prevent performance, the affected party promptly explains the effect, takes reasonable steps to minimise delay and proposes a revised date. Either party may close if an interruption continues for 30 days without an agreed restart. Jamie’s inability to continue and a client’s voluntary cancellation have the different financial consequences set out in section 5. Where neither party is at fault, only usable contracted outputs already delivered are charged at their applicable outcome fee. Refund unearned advances within 14 days. Remedies for breach remain available.

On closure Jamie hands over paid-for deliverables and accounts for payments. Closure does not require delivery of uncommissioned directions or unfinished drafts. Payment, refunds, accrued rights, confidentiality, data handling, intellectual property, liability and dispute provisions continue as needed to give them effect.

Send notices to the engagement email addresses in the scope, or a replacement address notified in writing. Keep copies of the accepted scope, terms, confirmations, delivery messages and closing statement. If a message bounces, resend using a working address and confirm receipt before relying on a deadline.

Scottish law governs this agreement and related non-contractual obligations. Subject to mandatory rights, the courts of Scotland have exclusive jurisdiction. Either party can give written notice of a dispute. The parties will seek resolution directly for up to 30 days before bringing proceedings. Either may seek urgent protective relief or take steps necessary to preserve a legal time limit sooner.

If a provision is unenforceable, the remainder continues to apply to the extent it can operate independently. The website may display later versions. The version accepted for an engagement continues throughout that engagement unless both parties expressly agree a written change.
