Terms of Engagement

Clear terms for a fixed-scope, written-first practice. These terms govern all engagements conducted through this website and related business communications.

Last updated: August 2026 — Jamie Forrester, Edinburgh, UK

Scope Three fixed-scope business-to-business written engagements — Structural Lens Brief, Diagnostic Artifact, or Full Structural Engagement. No ongoing retainer, no implementation.
Ownership The delivered artifact or artifact set is yours on full payment. 50% before work begins, 50% on delivery. IP in the underlying frameworks remains with Jamie Forrester.
Confidentiality All client materials are confidential. Not shared, referenced, or used as case study without explicit permission.
engagement terms
01 // Scope of services what is and is not included

What these engagements are

Jamie Forrester provides three fixed-scope written engagements: a Structural Lens Brief, a Diagnostic Artifact, and a Full Structural Engagement. The Structural Lens Brief produces one concise architecture-extraction artifact. The Diagnostic Artifact produces one structural diagnosis. The Full Structural Engagement produces four linked artifacts as one governed sequence: Translation Artifact, Diagnostic Artifact, Redesign Executive Summary, and Full Redesign Artifact.

Business-to-business basis. These services are intended for organisations and for individuals acting wholly or mainly for business or professional purposes. If you are seeking to engage as a consumer, contact Jamie Forrester before entering into an engagement because different statutory rights may apply. Nothing in these terms excludes or restricts any right or remedy that cannot lawfully be excluded or restricted.

The Structural Lens Brief is normally delivered within 7 days of engagement commencement. The Diagnostic Artifact is normally delivered within 14 days. The Full Structural Engagement operates within a standard 14-day engagement window: the Diagnostic Artifact is delivered first, followed by its clarification pass and written confirmation of the diagnostic frame; the remaining Full Structural Engagement artifacts are then released. Client-side delay in providing materials or confirming the diagnostic frame may extend final release beyond day 14. Any separately agreed scope may also specify a different timetable.

Engagements are confirmed only after a fit assessment. The scope of each engagement is agreed in writing before work begins. This agreement constitutes the full scope of the service.

Not included in any engagement

  • Implementation, build execution, or delivery ownership
  • Programme management or transformation leadership
  • Ongoing advisory, coaching, or retainer access
  • Stakeholder facilitation or alignment workshops
  • Legal, financial, regulatory, or compliance advice
  • Any work beyond the agreed written artifact
02 // Engagement process from brief to delivery

Intake and fit assessment

Recurring-failure enquiries normally begin with a five-line brief submitted by email. Structural Lens enquiries may begin with a simple request describing the system, problem, method, or body of work to be examined. Receipt of an enquiry does not constitute acceptance of an engagement. Jamie Forrester will assess fit and confirm or decline within a reasonable timeframe. There is no charge for the fit assessment stage.

Scope confirmation

If the engagement is accepted, the scope is confirmed in writing before work begins. The scope confirmation sets the boundary of the work. Any material change to the scope after confirmation requires a separate written agreement and may result in a new engagement.

Delivery

The Structural Lens Brief will normally be delivered within 7 days of engagement commencement. The Diagnostic Artifact will normally be delivered within 14 days. The Full Structural Engagement has a standard 14-day engagement window: the Diagnostic Artifact is delivered first; one clarification pass follows; the client then confirms in writing that the diagnostic frame is accepted as the basis for the redesign release; and the remaining Full Structural Engagement artifacts are released within the same standard window. If client-side confirmation or required materials are delayed, final release may extend beyond day 14. A separately agreed scope may specify a different timetable.

Clarification passes

The Structural Lens Brief includes one minor clarification pass. A standalone Diagnostic Artifact includes one minor clarification pass. A Full Structural Engagement includes one minor clarification pass on the Diagnostic Artifact before confirmation of the diagnostic frame and one minor clarification pass on the Full Redesign Artifact after its release. Clarification means explaining or correcting the delivered work within the agreed scope; it does not include new analysis, revised scope, extended research, implementation work, or additional deliverables. Any request outside this boundary may require a separate engagement.

03 // Fees and payment fixed fees, no surprises

Fee structure

The Structural Lens Brief is priced at a fixed fee of £2,000. The Diagnostic Artifact is priced at a fixed fee of £10,000. The Full Structural Engagement is priced at a standard fixed fee of £25,000 for most systems and includes the complete four-artifact governed sequence. Unusually large, multi-institutional, or high-consequence systems may be scoped separately; any different fee will be agreed in writing before work begins.

Fees are stated exclusive of VAT where applicable. Invoices will specify applicable VAT.

Payment terms

50% of the agreed fee is invoiced on scope confirmation and is due before work commences. The remaining 50% is invoiced on delivery of the agreed artifact or artifact set and is due within 14 days of delivery. Work will not begin until the first invoice is settled. The first payment is non-refundable once work has commenced.

Ownership of the delivered artifact or artifact set does not transfer until the second invoice is paid in full. Late payment of the second invoice may result in withholding of the delivered work until settlement.

Expenses

No expenses are incurred in the normal course of these engagements. All work is conducted remotely. If any out-of-pocket expenses are necessary, they will be agreed in writing in advance and invoiced at cost.

Currency

All fees are quoted and invoiced in British Pounds Sterling (GBP) unless otherwise agreed in writing.

Disputes

If you believe an invoice is incorrect, notify Jamie Forrester in writing within 7 days of receipt. Undisputed portions of an invoice remain due on the standard payment terms.

04 // Intellectual property ownership of the artifact and underlying frameworks

Ownership of the delivered work

Upon receipt of full payment, the client owns the delivered artifact or artifact set and may use it internally for any lawful purpose, including circulation to decision owners, builders, and advisers, and as a basis for implementation decisions, subject to any access or circulation restrictions expressly stated within a restricted artifact.

Underlying frameworks and methods

The intellectual property in the analytical frameworks, diagnostic methods, structural models, and proprietary concepts used to produce the artifact — including but not limited to the Four-Function Law, the Universal Formula, and any associated taxonomies or models — remains the property of Jamie Forrester. Ownership of the delivered work does not transfer ownership of the underlying methods.

No publication without permission

The artifact may not be published externally, reproduced in whole or in part for public distribution, used in marketing materials, or attributed publicly to Jamie Forrester without prior written permission. This restriction does not limit internal use.

No use as case study

No client materials, engagement details, or artifact content will be used as a case study, reference, or public example without the client's explicit prior written consent.

05 // Confidentiality mutual obligations

Treatment of client materials

All materials provided by the client — including documents, diagrams, contracts, workflows, internal records, and any other operational information — are treated as confidential. They are handled only to the extent necessary to assess, scope, and deliver the engagement.

Client materials are not shared with third parties, uploaded to public tools, used to train AI models, or referenced externally without explicit written permission. See also the Privacy Policy for data handling details.

Mutual expectation

Where Jamie Forrester shares working notes, draft materials, or interim documents during the engagement, these are also to be treated as confidential by the client and not shared externally without agreement.

Exceptions

Confidentiality obligations do not apply to information that is already in the public domain, was known to Jamie Forrester before the engagement, or is required to be disclosed by law or regulatory order.

No client materials are used to train AI tools. Where AI-assisted tools are used in the practice, client data is not submitted as input. Client materials are handled only for the purpose of the relevant engagement.

06 // Client obligations what the engagement requires from you

Materials and information

  • Provide accurate and complete materials relevant to the agreed scope
  • Respond to clarifying questions in a timely manner
  • Disclose known constraints, legal restrictions, or material limitations before work begins
  • Notify Jamie Forrester promptly if the scope or underlying facts change materially during the engagement

Accuracy of inputs

The quality of the artifact depends on the accuracy and completeness of the materials provided. Jamie Forrester is not liable for errors in the artifact that result from inaccurate, incomplete, or misleading information supplied by the client.

Hidden constraints or material facts discovered after delivery may affect the validity of the analysis. If this occurs, it will be treated as the basis for a new engagement, not a revision of the delivered artifact.

Nature of the service

The work produced may include structural architecture extraction, structural diagnosis, and redesign frameworks depending on the engagement selected. It represents professional judgment applied to the materials provided. It is not legal advice, financial advice, regulatory guidance, or a guarantee of outcome. Implementation decisions made on the basis of the delivered work remain the responsibility of the client.

Limitation of liability

Nothing in these terms limits or excludes liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence, fraud or fraudulent misrepresentation, or any other liability that cannot lawfully be limited or excluded. Subject to those exceptions and to the extent permitted by law, Jamie Forrester's total aggregate liability arising out of or in connection with an engagement — whether in contract, delict (including negligence), breach of statutory duty, misrepresentation, or otherwise — is limited to the total fees paid or payable for that engagement. Subject to the same exceptions, Jamie Forrester is not liable for indirect or consequential loss, or for loss of profit, revenue, business, opportunity, anticipated savings, goodwill, or implementation costs, except to the extent that such exclusion would be unlawful or unreasonable.

No warranty of outcome

No warranty is given that following the analysis or recommendations in the delivered work will produce a specific business outcome. Where an engagement includes redesign, the work defines a structural read and a path to change; it does not guarantee that the change will be executed successfully or that the failure will not recur.

Professional standard

Jamie Forrester warrants that the services will be performed with reasonable skill and care, consistent with the standard expected of an experienced independent systems architect.

08 // Termination ending an engagement

Termination by the client

The client may terminate an engagement at any time by written notice. The first payment (50% of the agreed fee) is non-refundable where work has commenced. If termination occurs after delivery, the full fee remains due.

Termination by Jamie Forrester

Jamie Forrester may terminate an engagement immediately by written notice if the client materially breaches these terms, fails to pay invoices on time, provides materially inaccurate or misleading information, or if the engagement becomes impractical due to factors outside reasonable control.

Effect of termination

On termination, any work product completed to date may be delivered at Jamie Forrester's discretion. Confidentiality obligations and IP provisions survive termination. Outstanding invoices for work completed remain due and payable.

Force majeure

Neither party is liable for failure to perform obligations where that failure results from circumstances beyond reasonable control, provided the affected party notifies the other promptly and takes reasonable steps to mitigate the impact.

09 // Governing law jurisdiction and disputes

Governing law

These terms and any non-contractual obligations arising out of or in connection with them are governed by the law of Scotland. Subject to any rights that cannot lawfully be excluded, the courts of Scotland have exclusive jurisdiction to determine any dispute arising out of or in connection with an engagement.

Dispute resolution

In the event of a dispute, both parties agree to attempt resolution in good faith through direct communication before initiating formal proceedings. Where direct resolution is not possible within 30 days of written notice of a dispute, either party may pursue formal resolution through the courts.

Entire agreement

These terms, together with the written scope confirmation for any specific engagement, constitute the entire agreement between the parties. They supersede all prior discussions, representations, or agreements relating to the subject matter.

Severability

If any provision of these terms is found to be unenforceable, it will be modified to the minimum extent necessary to make it enforceable. The remaining provisions continue in full force.

Updates to these terms

These terms may be updated from time to time. The version in force at the time of scope confirmation governs that engagement. The most current version will always appear on this page.
